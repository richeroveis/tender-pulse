# Traditional Artisan Sourdough Bread Recipe & Baking Guide
Category: Culinary Arts / Baking
Status: Not applicable to computer networking or blockchain infrastructure

## Ingredients
- 500g unbleached bread flour
- 350g lukewarm filtered water (70% hydration)
- 100g active sourdough starter (100% hydration)
- 10g fine sea salt

## Method
1. Autolyse: Combine flour and water in a large mixing bowl until no dry flour remains. Cover and let rest for 45 minutes.
2. Inoculate: Add active sourdough starter and dimple into dough with wet fingers.
3. Bulk Fermentation: Perform 4 sets of stretch-and-folds spaced 30 minutes apart over a 4-hour window at 26°C.
4. Shaping: Pre-shape into a round boule on an unfloured workbench. Rest 15 minutes, then perform final shaping into an oval batard.
5. Cold Retard: Place into a floured banneton basket and refrigerate overnight at 4°C for 14 hours.
6. Bake: Pre-heat Dutch oven to 240°C. Score dough, load with 2 ice cubes for steam, bake with lid on for 20 minutes, then lid off for 22 minutes until deep mahogany crust develops.
